# 19. Roadmap

| Next step | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Teacher review of the question bank | Turns 611 outcomes of draft material into trusted material. The single most valuable next step. |
| Complete Class XII | Four subjects remain; the same method applies. |
| Study commitments | A student sets a goal and NISAAB holds them to it, so it helps students who do not already open it. |
| Student contributions | Students offer their own notes, and a teacher approves before anything is shared. |
| Compulsory subjects | English, Urdu, Islamiat and Pakistan Studies. |
| Calibrated AI marking | Compare AI marks with a set of teacher-marked answers per subject. |
| Closed beta | Fifty to a hundred students from two or three colleges, before a wider launch. |
| Later features | Photo marking of handwritten answers, teacher and parent dashboards, an installable offline app, MDCAT and ECAT preparation, and other boards. |

A request has been made to the National Book Foundation for permission to use the text of the prescribed books. The method is not specific to FBISE: any board that publishes a syllabus document could be added in the same way.
