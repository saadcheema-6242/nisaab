# 4. User Guide: Getting Started

## 4.1 What you need

- A phone or computer with a web browser.
- An internet connection. NISAAB does not work offline.
- An email address and a password.

## 4.2 Signing up

1. Open the NISAAB link in your browser.
2. Sign up with your email and a password. Sign-in is handled by Google Identity Platform, so your password is never stored by NISAAB itself.

## 4.3 First-time setup

After signing up, a short setup asks three things.

| Question | Choices |
|---|---|
| Class | XI or XII |
| Group | Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering or ICS |
| Explanation language | English, Urdu or Roman Urdu |

Formulas and scientific terms stay in English whatever language you choose, because the exam is in English.

## 4.4 Your subject workspaces

NISAAB then creates one workspace for each subject in your group and shows them on the "My FSc" page. A Pre-Medical student gets Physics, Chemistry and Biology. A Pre-Engineering student gets Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics. An ICS student gets Physics, Mathematics and Computer Science. Each workspace holds its own syllabus map, chats, practice, papers and history.

## 4.5 A good first session

1. Open a subject and look at the syllabus map to see what is examined.
2. Tap one outcome and read the Explain panel.
3. Open the tutor and ask a question in your own words.
4. Ask for five questions on that topic and answer them.
5. Open **My progress** to see which outcomes are weak.
