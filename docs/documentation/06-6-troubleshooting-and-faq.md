# 6. Troubleshooting and FAQ

## 6.1 Common problems

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| The app is slow to open or shows an error the first time | The services may have just been switched on. Wait a few seconds and reload. Services are switched off between demonstrations to save credits. |
| A formula looks broken | Reload the chat. Formulas are repaired on screen and cleaned before saving. Report the question if it persists. |
| The tutor says an outcome is not examined | That outcome is formative or practical. It is taught but not asked in the theory paper. |
| Predicted grade says the estimate is not reliable | You have practised too little of the syllabus. Practise more outcomes and check again. |
| A numerical quiz takes long | Numericals that are not in the bank are written by the AI, which takes longer. Bank questions open in about 4 seconds. |
| Nothing loads at all | Check your internet connection. NISAAB needs it. |

## 6.2 Frequently asked questions

**Is NISAAB an official FBISE product?** No. It is a student project that uses FBISE's published documents.

**Can the tutor be wrong?** Yes. The model can still make mistakes inside the syllabus. Grounding controls what is answered, not whether the science is correct. That is why each answer cites its outcome and why nothing in the bank is marked approved until a teacher reviews it.

**Does it replace my teacher?** No. It is for practice and for questions you cannot ask in class.

**Is it free?** Yes, for students.

**Who can see my notes and answers?** Only you. Every record belongs to your own account, and deleting your account deletes your documents with it.
