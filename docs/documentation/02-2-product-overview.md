# 2. Product Overview

## 2.1 What NISAAB is

NISAAB is an AI study companion for students of the Federal Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education (FBISE). The word *nisaab* means syllabus in Urdu. The tutor answers only from FBISE's own published Assessment Frameworks, and every answer names the learning outcome it came from. That makes each answer checkable: a teacher can compare it with the official document.

Around the tutor, NISAAB offers a syllabus map, quizzes, flashcards, mind maps, full examination papers in the 2026 FBISE pattern, written-answer marking, mastery tracking and a predicted grade. It explains in English, Urdu or Roman Urdu and is free for students.

## 2.2 The problem it solves

Since the National Curriculum 2022-23, FBISE sets papers from Student Learning Outcomes (SLOs) instead of one textbook, and in August 2026 it moved to a pattern of 50% multiple choice and 50% descriptive questions. Students have limited help: a teacher has no time for individual questions, tuition costs money, and online material is usually for another board or an older syllabus. General AI tools know the subjects but not what the Federal Board examines, how deep to go or how marks are awarded. NISAAB closes that gap.

## 2.4 Coverage

| Subject | Outcomes loaded | Examined in the annual paper |
|---|---:|---:|
| Physics XI | 193 | 150 |
| Chemistry XI | 329 | 217 |
| Biology XI | 246 | 161 |
| Mathematics XI | 79 | 61 |
| Computer Science XI | 22 | 22 |
| **Total** | **869** | **611** |

These cover 34 content domains across all three FSc groups. Physics XII (151 outcomes, 2024 scheme) is also loaded. More than 4,000 practice items have been generated in advance: for each examined outcome, an explanation, five multiple choice questions and one short question. A separate bank of numerical questions covers the 191 outcomes in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics that are examined with calculations.

## 2.5 Scope

**Included:** Class XI for all three groups; Physics XII; the tutor, syllabus map, practice, papers, marking, progress and history; teacher review page.

**Not yet included:** Class XII for Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics and Computer Science; compulsory subjects (English, Urdu, Islamiat, Pakistan Studies); teacher review of the question bank; offline use.
