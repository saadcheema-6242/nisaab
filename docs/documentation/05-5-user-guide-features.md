# 5. User Guide: Features

## 5.1 Syllabus map

Each subject opens with its official syllabus, arranged by domain. For every outcome you see its code and text, its cognitive level (K, U or A) and a "Not examined" badge for outcomes that are only taught in class. A colour shows your mastery of that outcome and changes as you practise.

You can search and filter the map. Tapping an outcome opens a panel with four actions: **Explain**, **Practise MCQs**, **Flashcards** and **Written answer**.

## 5.2 The AI tutor

Type a question in English, Urdu or Roman Urdu. The tutor looks up the matching outcome, answers in the language you chose, shows formulas properly and names the outcome code, for example "SLO P-11-B-05". If the outcome is not examined in the theory paper, it tells you so. For Computer Science it writes Python 3 code in code blocks. The tutor can also check the FBISE website when you ask about announcements, date sheets or results.

| You type | What happens |
|---|---|
| *projectile ki range kaise nikalte hain?* | A Roman Urdu explanation with the formula, citing SLO P-11-B-05 |
| *is par 5 numericals do* | A quiz card of five numerical questions, taken from the bank in seconds |
| *vectors par quiz chahiye* | The tutor first asks a few quick questions, then makes the quiz |
| *cross product ke 5 numericals do, board level* | A clear request, so the quiz is made straight away |

## 5.3 Practice cards in the chat

- **Quiz card.** One question at a time. Tap an answer to see at once whether it is right, with an explanation. At the end you see your score and an "Ask tutor about my mistakes" button. "Try again" saves a new attempt.
- **Flashcards.** Tap to flip, then mark "Knew it" or "Still learning". Self-rated results count at half weight for mastery.
- **Mind maps.** A map of a whole chapter is drawn straight from the syllabus. A topic map has one branch per outcome with key points and formulas. You can zoom, open branches and tap a node for Explain or Practise.

## 5.4 Quick questions before practice

When your request is vague, the tutor shows a small card of three to five tappable questions. The first asks which part of the syllabus, using real outcome codes, with your weakest outcomes already ticked. Others ask for question type, difficulty and number of questions. Clear requests, repeats such as "ek aur", and "you decide" skip this step.

## 5.5 Examination papers

You can take a full or half paper in the FBISE 2026 pattern, built from examined outcomes. The paper has stimulus-based multiple choice sets and internal choice in Sections B and C. The exam page has a countdown timer, saves your answers automatically and submits by itself when time runs out. The structure is in section 12.1.

## 5.6 Written-answer marking

Type an answer to a short or extended question. NISAAB marks it point by point against a mark scheme, tells you what was missing, gives feedback in your language and shows a model answer. If a question offers a choice, only the part you answered is counted. Marks are labelled **AI-estimated**.

## 5.7 Progress and predicted grade

**My progress** shows your mastery by domain, your weakest outcomes with a Practise button, and a predicted grade band on the IBCC scale. The prediction comes with a coverage figure and a confidence label. If you have practised too little, the screen says the estimate is not reliable yet.

## 5.8 History and downloads

Every finished quiz, flashcard run and paper is saved as a numbered attempt (Attempt 1, Attempt 2 and so on) with its score, date and time. The History tab lists them newest first and each one opens again. The Download menu saves quizzes (with or without the answer key), flashcards (printable cards), mind maps (PNG or PDF) and notes (PDF). Downloads are drawn in the browser, so formulas and Urdu look as they do on screen.

## 5.9 Your own notes

You can upload your own notes or book as a PDF or DOCX file. NISAAB reads and indexes them, and the tutor searches them first when you ask a question. Your uploads are private to you and never enter the shared question bank.

## 5.10 Teacher review page

Teachers who are listed as reviewers get a **Review content** page. They can approve or reject each generated item and add a note. Students never see rejected items, and approved quizzes carry a "Teacher-checked" badge. The page is built, but the first review of the bank has not happened yet.
