Privacy Policy

NISAAB, an AI study assistant for FBISE FSc students. Last updated 7 November 2026.

This explains what NISAAB stores about you, why, and how to get rid of it. It is written to be read rather than to be impressive.

What we store

  • Your sign-in. Your email address, and either a password or the fact that you signed in with Google. Passwords are handled by Google Identity Platform and are never visible to us.
  • Your study profile. Your class, your group (Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering or ICS) and your preferred language, so the tutor follows the right syllabus.
  • What you make in the app. Chats with the tutor, notes, quizzes, flashcards, mind maps, practice papers and your answers to them.
  • Documents you upload. The file itself, the text taken out of it, and a numeric index of that text used to find relevant passages when you ask a question.
  • Community posts. What you post is visible to other students in your class and group. Your email address is never shown there.
  • Usage counts. How many messages, quizzes and uploads you have made today, so the daily limits can be applied.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • We do not ask for your phone number, your address, your ID card number or any payment details.
  • We do not show your email address to other students.

Where it lives

Everything is held in Google Cloud in the Mumbai region (asia-south1): the database, your uploaded files, and the sign-in records. Nothing is stored on anyone's personal computer.

The AI part

To answer a question, the relevant part of your work is sent to Google's Gemini models through Vertex AI. Google does not use Vertex AI content to train its models. Replies are generated by a model and can be wrong: NISAAB is a study aid, not a substitute for your textbook or your teacher.

Why we ask you to confirm your email

So that an account belongs to someone who can actually receive mail at that address. It keeps made-up accounts out and means we can reach you about your own account. We do not send marketing email.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account from Settings. That removes your profile, your projects and everything in them, your uploads and your community posts. It cannot be undone. If anything is left behind, write to us and we will remove it.

Students under 18

NISAAB is built for FSc students, so most people using it are at school. We collect no more than what is listed above. If you are a parent or guardian and want an account removed, write to us and we will do it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes with it.
